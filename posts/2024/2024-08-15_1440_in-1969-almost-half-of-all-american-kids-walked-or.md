In 1969, almost half of all American kids walked or biked to school. Today, that number is down to 10%

Why did this happen, and what are the consequences? 🧵

## 2/5

As the car-centric suburban experiment intensified, more and more parents became convinced that it was unsafe for their kids to walk or bike to school. So parents increasingly drove their kids, paradoxically adding even more traffic, making streets even less safe.

![[1824094056776921405-GVB7xgqX0AIiAIc.png]]

## 3/5

Another reason why kids don't walk anymore is because schools are located further away. Car-centric suburban sprawl, well... sprawls out the housing density. When you combine this with the phenomenon of building bigger schools, you end up with fewer schools that are further away.

![[1824094175454691507-GVB78WJX0AEIUm9.png]]
