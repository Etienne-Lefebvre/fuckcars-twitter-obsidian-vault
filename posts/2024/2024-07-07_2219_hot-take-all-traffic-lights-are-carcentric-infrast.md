## 1/5

🚨 Hot take 🚨

All traffic lights are car-centric infrastructure, therefore they shouldn't be used in cites.

![[1810076257121382640-GR6uoLRagAIRg4J.jpg]]

## 2/5

Cars shouldn't be going faster than 30km/h in cities. At those speeds, it's best just to use traffic calming if you are worried about safety. This is why traffic lights are fairly rare in Dutch cities.

*❤ 131 · 🔁 8*

## 3/5

The other problem with traffic lights is that it tells drivers that when the light is green "it's their turn to occupy the street". It makes drivers more emboldned to speed and to avoid paying attention to their surroundings.

*❤ 108 · 🔁 6*

## 4/5

A car driver is much more likely to drive slowly and pay attention to their surroundings when the default assumption is that pedestrians and cyclists have priority at all times.

*❤ 76 · 🔁 8*

## 5/5

A street filled with traffic lights gives drivers the impression that the goal is to drive as quickly from one traffic light to an other, like a race.  Once that light is green, something like a pedestrian is seen as an obstacle that merits being attended to by your road rage.
