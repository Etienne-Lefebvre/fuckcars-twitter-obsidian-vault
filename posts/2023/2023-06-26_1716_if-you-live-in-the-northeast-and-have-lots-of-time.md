## 1/2

If you live in the Northeast and have lots of time to waste 😂 I highly recommend taking Amtrak's Adirondack train from Montreal to NYC. 

It takes 11 hours end to end (70$), but it's super scenic and cozy. It's rarely over 50% capacity.
