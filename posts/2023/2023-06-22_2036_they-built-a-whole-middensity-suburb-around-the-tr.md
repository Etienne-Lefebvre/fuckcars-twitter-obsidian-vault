## 3/5

Houten is half the size of Albany and it has ridership of 3.5 million per year. If you adjust for population that's 10 times more passengers than Albany. People use the train in Houten because its convenient and because there's lots of capacity.

## 4/5

If Houten was surrounded by parking lots and most people had to drive to use the train station, it absolutely would significantly reduce ridership even if you kept the capacity constant. People place an ENORMOUS value on convenience when it comes to transportation.

## 5/5

You will never be able to achieve high ridership levels if your train stations are surrounded by parking lots. The majority of people will be too inconvenienced to drive and then take a train, they will just end up driving the whole way.
